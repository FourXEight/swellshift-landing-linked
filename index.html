When the weather changes, plans change

Get cancelled lessons back on the calendar.

SwellShift is a simple, phone-first rebooking tracker concept for independent surf schools in Ireland. Keep cancelled bookings visible, find a suitable new session, and see the lesson value still waiting to be recovered.

A focused workflow in three steps

Designed around the busy moments after a surf session has to be cancelled.

1

Cancel the session

Use the Today view to cancel a session and move its sample bookings into the rebooking list.

2

Choose a new slot

See upcoming sessions with enough free places for the group and move the booking with one tap.

3

See what is at risk

Check the number of bookings still waiting and the illustrative euro value attached to them.

Built for a surf-school day

A small, clear tool concept rather than a full booking platform.

Phone-first controlsLarge tap targets and a simple three-view layout for quick use on a phone.
48-hour attention flagBookings waiting 48 hours or more are marked red in the demo.
Capacity-aware movesThe demo only offers sessions with enough free places for the group size.
